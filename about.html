About Meridian

Senior-Led Consultants, Built for Execution.

Meridian Consulting Group is a senior-led operational consulting firm. We don't sell slide decks — we embed with leadership teams to rewire core processes, land structural change, and prove results against agreed baselines.

Our Story

Built by Operators, Not Slide-Makers.

Meridian was founded in 2011 by a small group of senior operators who had spent their careers inside the organisations they were now consulting to. They'd seen the pattern — good strategy, sharp decks, and then nothing changing.

So they built a different kind of firm. One that starts with diagnosis, embeds with teams, and measures success by what actually changed on the floor — not by how many slides were shipped.

"If it doesn't land in the business, it doesn't count as consulting. That's still the rule we hire by."

— Founding Partner, Meridian CG

What Guides Us

Our Mission & Vision.

Two simple statements that shape every engagement — and how we decide who we work with.

Our Mission

To help leadership teams translate strategy into operational reality — through rigorous diagnosis, embedded execution, and measurable outcomes that hold long after we leave.

  • Outcome before activity
  • Senior-led, always
  • Capability transferred, not retained

Our Vision

A world where consulting is judged by measurable change — not by the elegance of the deliverables. We want every leadership team we work with to be stronger, faster, and more capable after we leave.

  • Measurable impact on every engagement
  • Trusted advisor, not a vendor
  • Capability that compounds in-house
Our Journey

Fifteen Years of Execution.

From a three-person boutique to a trusted partner for leadership teams across 18+ industries.
