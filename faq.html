General Questions

Working With Meridian.

The essentials — how we engage, who's on the team, and what to expect.

We work with mid-market to enterprise organisations — typically £50M to £2B+ in revenue — where operations are complex enough to merit a senior team. Our clients are usually facing a process reset, a structural change, or a turnaround. We've worked across manufacturing, healthcare, financial services, retail, logistics, technology and energy.

Most engagements run 8–16 weeks. A diagnostic phase takes 2–4 weeks; execution follows. Some engagements extend into a handover phase where we coach your team to own the change. We don't bill for long orientation periods — we aim to reach first measurable result in 6–10 weeks.

Every engagement is led by a partner or senior consultant who stays on the work start to finish. There is no junior hand-off. This is the single biggest difference between us and traditional consulting firms — the person who wins the work is the person who does the work.

Before work begins, we agree a baseline: the specific metrics we're improving, measured with data your finance or ops team signs off on. Every engagement is tracked against that baseline. We report progress on a live dashboard, not a slide at the end. If we don't hit the target, we stay until we do.

Services & Delivery

How We Actually Deliver.

Practical questions about our services, methods, and what you get out the other side.

Process optimization is the discipline of redesigning how the work flows — removing waste, cutting cycle time, releasing capacity. Change management is the discipline of landing that redesign inside the organisation — mapping stakeholders, coaching leadership, tracking adoption. In practice you need both: a great process nobody follows is just a slide. We run them together.

A diagnostic is 2–4 weeks of structured assessment. We interview the people doing the work, review the data, map the actual process (not the documented one), and surface the friction points that matter most. You get a prioritised improvement roadmap, agreed KPIs, and an honest view of what's achievable — before any implementation work begins.

In fact, one of our principles is that the capability stays in-house after we leave — we build the change muscle inside your organisation, not around it. In some engagements we recommend structural adjustments based on what we find, but that's a diagnosis output, not a precondition.

It means we don't get paid to produce slideware — we get paid to move a specific metric, agreed with you at the start. Every engagement begins with a baseline (cycle time, operating cost, adoption rate, throughput, etc.) and we report against it on a live dashboard. If the metric doesn't move, we don't call it a success.

Resistance is almost always a symptom of two things: leaders feeling excluded from the change, and leaders lacking capability to lead it. We work directly on both — early stakeholder mapping, structured 1:1 coaching for the managers who need to lead the shift, and a clear change narrative they can own. Change that lands is a leadership outcome, not a comms plan.

Every engagement ends with a handover pack: the redesigned process flows, role & responsibility maps, a change playbook, the delivery dashboard (live and yours), leadership coaching materials, and a documented improvement backlog. All owned by your team, not us. We transfer capability, not dependency.
